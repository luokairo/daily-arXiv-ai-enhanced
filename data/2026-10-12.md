### [1] [Decoding Register Tokens Preserves Global Scene Details](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13299)
*David Jacob Drexlin, Julius Hense, Jonas Dippel*

Main category: cs.CV, cs.LG

Direction: 视频/多模态世界模型与表征学习

Subtopic: RAE 与生成潜空间

Importance: 高优先级 (90.62) - 未入选精读

TL;DR: 在 RAE 解码器中保留并预测 register token 特征，可显著降低 ImageNet-1K 重建与生成 FID。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有基于 patch 的图像解码器丢弃了非空间 register token，而这些 token 被认为编码光照、模糊、风格等全局场景属性，作者因此探究在完整 patch 表征已具备时保留 register 特征是否仍能提升解码。

Method: 使用冻结的带 register 编码器构建 RAE，解码器通过联合自注意力同时处理四个 register token 与全部 patch token；并用外观探针、配对重建误差分析和固定解码器 register 替换来定位全局属性；生成阶段训练多头注意力池化（MHAP）预测器从采样 patch 恢复 register 特征，保持预训练扩散模型及其采样不变。

Result: ImageNet-1K 上 ViT-B 解码器平均重建 FID 为 DINOv2 0.472、DINOv3 0.442，相对各自 patch-only 基线降低 9.5% 与 5.2%，PSNR 为 19.73 与 20.54 dB；生成方面 DINOv2 模型平均 FID 1.641，在相同采样 patch 上比 patch-only 基线低 9.8%。

Conclusion: 保留 register token 特征能让解码器获取 patch 之外的全局场景信息，从而在重建与生成上稳定优于仅用 patch 的 RAE 基线。

Classification reason: 论文研究 RAE 解码器如何利用冻结 DINOv2/DINOv3 编码器的 register token 改善重建与生成，属于视觉自编码器与生成潜空间表征贡献，归 world_model 的 representation_autoencoder。

Importance reason: Preliminary abstract-based model judgment (70%) and personal heuristic (30%). Directly studies representation autoencoders (RAEs) with frozen DINOv2/v3 encoders, decoding register tokens for global scene details—core RAE and visual representation learning contribution.

Abstract: Pretrained vision transformers learn structured image representations spanning spatial patch tokens and non-spatial tokens. Recent analyses suggest that non-spatial register tokens encode global scene properties such as illumination, blur, and style. Yet patch-based image decoders discard these outputs, leaving the decoder to recover image appearance from patches alone. We investigate whether retaining register features improves decoding when the complete spatial patch representation is already available. We study this question using representation autoencoders (RAEs) with frozen register-equipped encoders. Our decoder processes four register tokens together with all patch tokens through joint self-attention. Appearance probes, paired reconstruction-error analysis, and fixed-decoder register substitutions examine which global image properties are accessible from registers and how their values affect reconstructed pixels. For generation, we train a multi-head attention pooling (MHAP) predictor to recover register features from sampled patches, keeping the pretrained diffusion model and its samples unchanged. On ImageNet-1K, our ViT-B decoders achieve mean reconstruction FIDs of 0.472 with DINOv2 and 0.442 with DINOv3, reductions of 9.5% and 5.2% relative to their respective patch-only baselines. The corresponding PSNRs are 19.73 and 20.54 dB. For generation, our DINOv2 models achieve a mean FID of 1.641, 9.8% below the patch-only baseline on identical sampled patches.

### [2] [Learning Representations to Guide Pixel Diffusion Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12978)
*Zihao Pan, Mengting Chen, Cheng Shi, Taihang Hu, Zhengrong Yue, Zikang Wang, Yuan Wang, Wei Li, Sibei Yang*

Main category: cs.CV

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 视觉表征多面向理解任务，其对生成是否有用尚不明确；作者发现外部对齐语义在像素合成中会逐渐丢失，且架构分离与专用像素解码器只是重新分配语义与空间建模，未必产生更适合生成的表征。

Method: 提出 Representation Guidance（RG），在保持朴素 Transformer 架构下，用低维瓶颈把与生成相关的信息压缩为紧凑内部表征，并依据其诱导的预测偏移修改 flow-matching 目标，从而显式引导解码器生成。

Result: ImageNet 256x256 上 RG-L/16 仅 459M 参数即超越更大的像素扩散 Transformer，RG-XL/16 达到 FID 1.55；512x512 下 FID 1.74，并扩展到文生图，GenEval 0.89、DPG-Bench 87.1。

Conclusion: 有用的生成表征必须既被正确形成又被主动利用；RG 学到更清晰的语义与空间结构，并在参数与计算上高效扩展。

Classification reason: 论文研究像素扩散 Transformer 中何种视觉表征对生成有用，并提出低维瓶颈形成内部表征以引导生成，属于生成潜空间/表征对齐研究，归 world_model 的 RAE 与生成潜空间主题；虽为图像侧，但该主题明确包含图像侧基础研究。

Importance reason: Preliminary abstract-based model judgment (70%) and personal heuristic (30%). Studies what makes visual representations useful for generation in pixel diffusion transformers, introducing Representation Guidance with a compact latent bottleneck guiding flow-matching targets—directly relevant to RAE/generative latent representation learning.

Abstract: Visual representations are largely developed for understanding, yet what makes them useful for generation remains unclear. We study this question in pixel diffusion transformers and uncover two key insights. First, better representation quality does not necessarily translate into greater generative utility, as externally aligned semantics can be progressively lost during pixel synthesis. Second, architectural separation with specialized pixel decoders mainly redistributes semantic and spatial modeling, without necessarily yielding representations better suited to generation. These insights suggest that a useful generative representation must be both properly formed and actively exploited. Motivated by these insights, we introduce Representation Guidance (RG), which directly forms and exploits internal representations while retaining a plain Transformer architecture. A low-dimensional bottleneck forces generation-relevant information into a compact representation, which is then explicitly used to guide decoder generation by modifying the flow-matching target according to its induced prediction offset. RG learns clearer semantic and spatial structure while scaling efficiently in both parameters and computation. On ImageNet 256x256, RG-L/16 with only 459M parameters outperforms substantially larger pixel diffusion transformers, while RG-XL/16 achieves an FID of 1.55. RG further reaches an FID of 1.74 at 512x512 and extends to text-to-image generation, achieving 0.89 on GenEval and 87.1 on DPG-Bench.
