  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: DeltaRAE 是因果表征自编码器：周期性锚帧完整编码，其余帧以少量 token 编码其相对变化；因果编码器与解码器读取冻结的预训练图像编码器。

Classification reason: 提出因果视频表征自编码器与导数式 tokenization，属 RAE/视频 tokenizer 表征贡献，直接归 world_model 的 representation_autoencoder；其长视频生成与 token 效率也服务视频生成。

Importance reason: Preliminary abstract-based model judgment (70%) and personal heuristic (30%). Abstract: Latent video generators are trained on clips of a few seconds, and much of this limit lies in their tokenizers, which spend as many tokens on a static frame as on a moving one and need future frames that do not exist during generation.
