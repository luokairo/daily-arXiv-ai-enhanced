Result: 理论上证明"信任最新时间戳"成立的充要条件、无回退后视图时只有动作日志可区分回退区域，以及生成器可自预测时重标注优于删除；实验上 FAR 显示当前状态的比例由 0.36 升至 0.62，HY-WorldPlay 由 0.003 升至 0.19，并提出校准的状态度量。

Conclusion: 时间戳语义（有效时间）而非新旧顺序才是交互式视频世界模型记忆的关键，免训练的重标注可显著改善撤销后的状态一致性，并提供可复用的评测指标。

Classification reason: 针对交互式视频世界模型的记忆机制（有效时间标注）提出免训练方法，属视频世界模型的长时序/交互记忆建模；涉及智能体动作日志，故也匹配应用方向。

Importance reason: Preliminary abstract-based model judgment (70%) and personal heuristic (30%). Proposes valid-time memory re-dating for interactive video world models, a core memory/state contribution to action-conditioned video world models.

With frames, weights and noise fixed, reversing the timestamps of two views flips the state that generators of two families draw: they take the newest timestamp as the truth. That reading is right as long as states never return. After an agent undoes an action, the newest view of a region is outdated and the only view of the present is older; memories for changing worlds delete that older view, and memories that keep it hand the generator the past.
