### [1] [LOCI: Spatial Linear Memory for Streaming World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40222)
*Ji Xia, Tingting Liao, Xuezhi Liang, Hao Li, Guangyi Liu*

Main category: cs.CV

Direction: 世界模型

Subtopic: 视频世界模型

Importance: 高优先级 (92.19) - Top 1 精读候选

TL;DR: LOCI 用相机几何条件化的循环线性注意力记忆与 KV 缓存混合，使流式视频世界模型在恒定显存下更忠实地重建重访视角内容。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 视频世界模型在相机重访已观测区域时应重现原有内容，这既需要记住过去观测，也需要按当前视角检索到正确的观测。KV 缓存保留细节但随视频长度增长，循环记忆紧凑却把历史压缩为固定状态、无法直接访问单次观测。

Method: 提出混合空间记忆架构 LOCI：一半 transformer 块保留过去观测的 KV 缓存，另一半仅关注当前 chunk 并辅以循环线性注意力记忆，其读写由投影相机几何条件化，使视角同时进入记忆寻址与存储内容；循环读出流入后续缓存块，为其 query 提供累积场景上下文。

Result: 在公开 MIND 记忆基准与留存的录制轨迹上，LOCI 对重访内容的重建比代表性世界模型和同配方全 softmax 模型更忠实；在完整历史下，等长度时峰值显存比全 softmax 降低约 30%；在有限保留观测的预算下可恒定显存流式处理长视频，且同预算下仍优于全 softmax。

Conclusion: 将几何条件化的循环线性记忆与 KV 缓存结合，可在保持细节可检索性的同时实现恒定显存的长视频流式世界建模，并提升重访视角的一致性。

Classification reason: 面向视频世界模型的长时序记忆与视角重访一致性，属视频世界模型；其流式长视频记忆机制也服务长视频生成。

Importance reason: Preliminary abstract-based model judgment (70%) and personal heuristic (30%). Directly addresses video world models with spatial memory for revisiting observed regions, a core world-model capability; hybrid KV-cache plus recurrent linear attention conditioned on camera geometry.

Abstract: When a camera revisits a previously observed region, a video world model should reproduce what was there before. This requires both remembering past observations and retrieving the right one for the current viewpoint. Key-value caches preserve visual detail but grow with video length; recurrent memory is compact but compresses history into a fixed-size state, so individual past observations are no longer directly accessible. We introduce LOCI, a hybrid spatial-memory architecture that keeps both representations. In half of the transformer blocks, main attention keeps a key-value cache of past observations; in the other half, it is restricted to the current chunk and complemented by a recurrent linear-attention memory whose reads and writes are conditioned on projective camera geometry, so viewpoint enters both memory addressing and stored content. Recurrent readouts flow into subsequent cache-backed blocks and supply their queries with accumulated scene context. On the public MIND memory benchmark and on held-out recorded trajectories, LOCI reproduces revisited content more faithfully than representative world models and a same-recipe full-softmax model; with full history, it lowers peak memory at equal length by about 30% relative to full softmax. With a bounded bank of retained observations, it streams long videos at constant memory and remains more faithful than full softmax under the same budget.
